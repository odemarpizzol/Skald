- **Battlegrounds:** a match won or lost becomes a page of its own (Darkspear Islands and the older fields), in English, Portuguese and French; three new goals for matches won (1, 10 and 50). Diaries that are already past them mark them quietly. The page is found under "Battles" in Search and shows in the Reader. (Not seen in a real Forever match yet.)
- **Skyborne:** Forever's new race has lines of its own for new lands, levels and falls, and the Saga names the isle where a Skyborne character was born (the map is kept when the diary sees the character at level 1 or 2)
- **Forever:** a boss whose outcome or id the game withholds from addons in a fight, and a loot line it withholds, are skipped quietly instead of raising an error in the chat
- **Forever:** the zone card counts the quests "for the Alliance" or "for the Horde" for a Skyborne character (the quest data only knows the eight old races)
- `/skald probe events` (a developer tool): counts every event the client fires and keeps the payload of the ones with a new-looking name (camps, Legacy, PvP...), for a report; `/skald probe events list` shows them, `/skald probe events off` stops it
