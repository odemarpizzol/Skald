- **Skyborne:** Forever's new race has lines of its own for new lands, levels and falls (only what is known of it: the wind, the air, the elements and the isle; nothing invented of its lore), and the Saga names the isle where a Skyborne character was born (the map is kept when the diary sees the character at level 1 or 2)
- **Forever:** a boss whose outcome or id the game withholds from addons in a fight, and a loot line it withholds, are skipped quietly instead of raising an error in the chat
- **Forever:** the zone card counts the quests "for the Alliance" or "for the Horde" for a Skyborne character (the quest data only knows the eight old races)
- `/skald probe events` (a developer tool): counts every event the client fires and keeps the payload of the ones with a new-looking name (camps, Legacy, PvP...), for a report; `/skald probe events list` shows them, `/skald probe events off` stops it
- **Lore of Forever:** in the Forever client the Bard leaves out the six pieces of a later time (Outland, Eversong, Azuremyst); the other clients keep all 33. A check now fails if any text of the diary names something of a later time (Pandaria, the Cataclysm, Outland, Garrosh...)
- **A new voice, "Of My People"** (Style tab), the voice of a first install: your race speaks (the time of Forever: Thrall's new Horde, the Forsaken free of the Lich King, Hyjal and the immortality given up, lost Gnomeregan, the Darkspear, the Earth Mother...), with no number of level and no points on the pages. A level is told in the words of your people (eight races, and a plain set for the others), a new land or a fall in the voice of your race three times in four. Choosing it tells the old level pages again. Whoever already had Skald keeps the voice they chose. The pools of new lands for each race go from 3 to 8 lines
