- **Pets:** the family of a pet saved in another language (a pet met in an English client, now in a Portuguese one) is shown in the language of the client.
- **Goal points:** the Goals tab grows from 12 to 46 goals in eight groups (the road, exploration, stories, war, crafts, beasts, company, fortune and renown), with levels, lands, quests, dungeons, bosses, creatures, trades, guild, group, gold and reputation. Each goal is worth points by the rarity of its seal (5, 10, 25, 50, 100); the tab shows the points earned, the title they earn (in the form of your character) and the value of each goal, the cover has a line with the total, the page of a goal reached says its points, and the Reader cover shows them. Goals that came in this version are marked quietly on the first look, so no wave of pages and notices appears.
- **Notices:** the Notices tab has a choice of how long a notice stays (4, 6, 10, 15 seconds or until clicked).
- **Goal points, art:** a gem beside the points of each goal and the cover, and an emblem for each of the six titles beside the title in the Goals tab.
- **Bug reports:** `/skald report` opens a block of text to copy with the version, language, page counts, the events this client fired and the ones never seen, and the last errors caught (no character name, no page text).
- **Developers:** `/skald probe comms` tests whether addons can talk to each other in this client (one tiny message on say, group and guild); the result appears in `/skald report`. Nothing is sent unless the command is typed.
- **Companions in a realmless world:** players are told apart by name and surname (the part the client gives as the realm), so two players with the same first name are two companions; the pages still tell only the name, and the export hides both.
