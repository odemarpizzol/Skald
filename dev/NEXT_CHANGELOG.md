- **PvP** tab (JxJ in Portuguese, JcJ in French): a war chronicle of your battlegrounds, with the matches won and lost in all and by field, the newest matches (a click opens the page in the book) and the game's own count of honorable kills when the client keeps one. It shows itself once a battleground is written, on any type of realm; `/skald pvp on|off|auto` decides it. The open world and arenas are not told yet. `/skald probe realm` lists what the client says about the realm, for the next step
